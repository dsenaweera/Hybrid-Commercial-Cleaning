Common questions

Commercial cleaning FAQs

What areas of Melbourne do you service?

Hybrid Commercial Services covers Melbourne and the surrounding suburbs. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, get in touch and we'll confirm before quoting.

Do you offer after-hours or weekend office cleaning?

Yes. Most of our commercial clients are cleaned before or after business hours, or on weekends, so cleaning never disrupts your team or customers. Scheduling is flexible around your operating hours.

What's included in a standard commercial office clean?

A standard office clean covers desks, common areas, kitchens, and bathrooms — vacuuming, mopping, dusting, surface sanitising, and rubbish removal. Add-ons like window cleaning or carpet care can be scheduled alongside your regular visit.

Are your commercial cleaners insured and background-checked?

Yes. Every cleaner is background-checked, trained, and covered by insurance, so your business, staff, and premises are protected on every visit.

How much does commercial cleaning cost in Melbourne?

Commercial and office cleaning starts from around $120 per visit, depending on the size of the space, how often you need cleaning, and the specific tasks involved. Request a free quote for an exact price.
